SRR — Re-teaming Parameters

SRR forms new teams from a class roster with a SoSAR Team column. Constraints are fixed: no Phase-1 teammates may share a Phase-2 team, and minority gender per team must be 0, 2, or 3 (never exactly 1).

SDR — Algorithm Notes

SDR re-teaming uses a v3 roster (SoSAR + SRR Team columns) and runs a conflict-pair-aware arrangement that minimizes problem placements and balances Air/Space preferences. No tunable parameters are surfaced for SDR in this version — the algorithm's internal defaults are used.

If you want to expose Phase-3 controls (e.g. team size, conflict-pair list, Air/Space ratio), tell Aaryan and they'll be added here.
